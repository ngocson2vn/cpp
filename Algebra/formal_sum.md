# Formal Sum
Given two vector spaces $W_1$ and $W_2$. The **External Direct Sum** between them is $W = W_1 \oplus W_2$.

Now that for every vector $w \in W$, we can express it as $w = w_1 \oplus w_2$ where $w_1 \in W_1$ and $w_2 \in W_2$.

In the expression $w = w_1 \oplus w_2$, the symbol $\oplus$ denotes the formation of an element of the external direct sum $W = W_1 \oplus W_2$. 

The resulting object $w$ is often called a *formal sum*.
